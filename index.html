Diffusion Language Models:
Foundations, Efficiency, and Reasoning

The first NeurIPS workshop dedicated to diffusion language models

Read the Call for Papers → Meet the Invited Speakers

Workshop focus

From foundations to intelligence

01 · Foundations

Theory & Scaling

Unifying diffusion formulations, scaling laws, training objectives, probabilistic inference, and model architectures.

02 · Performance

Efficiency

Parallel decoding, fast sampling, long-context generation, serving, evaluation, and hardware-aware systems.

03 · Intelligence

Reasoning

Global self-correction, diverse solution paths, test-time scaling, planning, code generation, and RL post-training.
