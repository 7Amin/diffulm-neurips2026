Format & presentation

Preparing your poster

Posters are presented in person at the workshop in Sydney. Workshop posters must be 61 cm wide × 85 cm high, in portrait format. Do not print your poster larger than this. Note that this differs from the main conference format (140 × 90 cm landscape).

There is no official workshop poster template, so any tool works (PowerPoint, Keynote, Canva, or LaTeX packages such as tikzposter or a0poster) as long as the page is set to the size above.

At least one author should present the poster in person. If none of the authors can attend, please arrange for someone else to present the paper on your behalf.

On the day

Display rules in Sydney

  • Posters go on the allocated poster boards near the workshop room
  • Do not hang posters on the venue walls, including inside the workshop room
  • Use only the venue-approved command strips, available in the workshop room
  • One strip per corner of your poster
  • Take your poster down right after your session so the boards are free for the next one

Optional printing service

Print and pick up in Sydney

NeurIPS offers poster printing through its official vendor, with pickup at the Exhibition Centre, Level 2, Registration Desk 3. The advance ordering deadline is November 4, 11:00 PM AEST, and NeurIPS will announce the regular deadline later.
